**Label meanings**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **REAL** | Genuine observational or reanalysis data from the named provider |
| **BENCHMARK** | Real data curated into a public research dataset with ground truth |
| **SYNTHETIC** | Generated by us, statistically grounded in real distributions |

> **The 1/12° (~8 km) grid is a physical floor on origin-field tightness**, not a modelling
> deficiency. State it openly; report `forcing_sensitivity` per case.

**Correlated-source warning (threat S-07):** CMEMS and ERA5 are not fully independent —
ocean reanalyses are forced by atmospheric reanalyses from the same lineage. Their
agreement is weaker evidence than it appears. The source dependency registry must record
this, and ensemble members should be drawn from **structurally different** products where
possible.

### Indian AIS — the honest position

Indian operational AIS is restricted/commercial and not obtainable by a student team. **The
PS explicitly permits synthetic data:** *"Real AIS if available may be used else synthetic
data can be prepared for the region of oil spill to demonstrate the functioning of the
algorithm."*

Our approach:
1. **Develop and validate on real US AIS** — real traffic geometry, real speed
   distributions, real gap statistics.
2. **Demonstrate on synthetic Indian-EEZ traffic** drawn from those same distributions.
3. **Say so on the slide, before anyone asks.** The path integral is trajectory-format
   agnostic; swapping feeds is configuration, not architecture.

## 5. Attribution ground truth — does not exist

> **No public dataset pairs a confirmed oil spill with a confirmed guilty vessel. Anywhere.**

This is precisely why the contribution is unclaimed, and it is why evaluation must be
simulation-based:

```
real AIS track → simulated release at a known point/time → advect forward under REAL
forcing → render slick → hand the system only the slick + surrounding traffic
```

Truth is known by construction; difficulty is a dial.

**Honesty constraint:** this measures whether the *inference* is sound given the physics.
It cannot measure whether the physics matches the real Bay of Bengal. Those are different
claims and must never be conflated.

> ⚠️ The existing harness in `experiments/go_nogo/` **does not meet this standard** — see
> `FAILURE_THREAT_MATRIX.md` §8. Its numbers must not be used.

## 6. Data layers

```
data/raw/       immutable, exactly as retrieved, never modified   ← PRAMAN references these
data/bronze/    parsed and validated, provenance attached
data/silver/    normalised units/CRS, gaps annotated
data/gold/      analysis-ready (tiles, tracks, forcing grids)
data/manifests/ dataset definitions + split manifests + content hashes
```

**Rule:** `data/raw/` is append-only. Every derived artifact references its raw parents via
`provenance.raw_artifact_refs`. Nothing in `data/` is committed to git.
